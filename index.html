Game of The Year (2026): GTA VI or Zelda Remake?

It is undebatable that this year for the gaming community was
a banger, especially on this month of November, having two
big releases, being GTA VI and Zelda Ocarina of Time: Remake.
But we have the question that arrives in the end of the year:
which one of those two will win GOTY?
Firstly I want to make it clear: I will not talk about the other
games that were nominated simply because they do not stand a realistic
chance of winning against the most awaited release of the year and a
remake of the best game of history.
Coming back to the main topic.
Analyzing the past winners of GOTY, mainly 2024 where Astrobot won.
We all know that Black Myth Wukong was supposed to win there, but because
of something, Astrobot won. We all know what happened. Someone received a
paycheck on their wallets and boom. It will not be different with GTA VI,
adding that it simply had too much hype. That's it.
